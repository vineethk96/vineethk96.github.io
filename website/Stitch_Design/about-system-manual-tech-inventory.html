PHASE_01: INDUSTRIAL_RIGOR

Beginning in the strict domain of Embedded Systems, my early architectural work focused on the unyielding reliability of UAV flight controllers and precision medical diagnostic devices. Here, a semicolon wasn't just syntax; it was a safety protocol. This foundation instilled a "build-to-last" philosophy that remains the core of every system I deploy.

PHASE_02: THE_PIVOT

The transition to Creative Technology at UCL represented a hardware-software synthesis. It was the realization that engineering rigor provides the skeleton, but human-centric design provides the soul. Today, I bridge the gap between high-level architectural performance and the tactile, immersive experiences of connected environments.
